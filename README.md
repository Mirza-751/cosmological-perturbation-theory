# Cosmological perturbations in synchronous and Newtonian gauges

**Full report**: [View PDF](theoretical_cosm_mustafa.pdf)

## Summary
A formal treatment of linear cosmological perturbation theory, following Ma & Bertschinger (1995). Covers:
- FLRW background and SVT decomposition
- Synchronous gauge (Lifshitz) and conformal Newtonian (longitudinal) gauge
- Gauge transformations and Bardeen potentials
- Boltzmann/fluid equations for CDM, baryons, photons, neutrinos
- Superhorizon solutions, acoustic oscillations, Silk damping, ISW effect
- Numerical implementation (CAMB/CLASS)

## Applications
Connects theory to CMB power spectra, matter power spectrum, and constraints on neutrino mass and dark energy.
